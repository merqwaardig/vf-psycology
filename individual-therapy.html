Individual therapy

Do you often feel overwhelmed by your emotions — or simply stuck, and unsure of what to do next?

Support that is personal to you

You don’t have to figure everything out on your own.

Life can feel like a lot sometimes. You may be going through a difficult period, facing a big change, or noticing the same thoughts, emotions, or situations coming up again and again.

Individual therapy gives you space to slow down, talk things through, and better understand what is happening for you. You don’t need to arrive with all the answers — we can explore things together, at a pace that feels comfortable for you.

Sometimes you may know exactly what you want help with. Other times, you might simply feel that something is not quite right. Both are completely valid reasons to start therapy.

A Different Perspective

You Are More Than What You’re Going Through

Therapy isn’t just about working through challenges. It’s also an opportunity to recognize your strengths, reconnect with what truly matters to you, and discover new ways to move forward with greater clarity and confidence.

What we can work on

Support shaped around what matters to you.

Anxiety & stress

Understand your worries and triggers, reduce the feeling of overwhelm, and develop practical ways to manage anxiety and stress — so you feel calmer, more grounded, and better able to handle difficult moments.

Self-esteem & confidence

Explore self-doubt, negative self-talk, perfectionism, or the feeling that you’re not good enough, and build greater self-understanding, self-compassion, and a more confident relationship with yourself.

Emotions & coping

Learn to understand your emotions rather than feeling controlled or overwhelmed by them, and find healthier ways of coping with difficult feelings, responding to stress, and caring for your emotional well-being.

Relationships & boundaries

Explore patterns in your relationships, improve communication, and express your needs more clearly — while building healthier boundaries that protect your time, energy, and well-being.

Low Mood & Depression

Explore feelings of sadness, low motivation, or emotional disconnection. Together, we can work toward understanding what you’re experiencing, developing supportive coping strategies, and finding manageable ways to move forward.

Life Changes & Finding Direction

Navigate personal, academic, or professional challenges, career decisions, and periods of uncertainty. Therapy can help you explore your priorities, gain clarity, and approach life’s transitions with greater confidence.

Your therapy, your way

Therapy that adapts to you.

There is no one-size-fits-all approach to therapy. Your experiences, personality, background, and goals are unique, so our sessions are shaped around what feels most helpful for you. Some sessions may involve talking and reflecting; others may include practical strategies, exercises, or different ways of looking at a situation.

Therapy is something we do together. You are always welcome to ask questions, share what is or isn’t working for you, and help decide what we focus on — and we can check in regularly and adjust along the way.

Getting started

Your Therapy Journey, Step by Step.

  1. A First Conversation

    Taking the first step can feel challenging. Start by completing the contact form to request a free 10-minute introductory call. I’ll get back to you with available times so we can briefly discuss what brings you here, answer your initial questions, and explore whether working together feels right for you.

    Starts with a free 10-minute call

  2. Getting to Know You

    Our first sessions are an opportunity to explore your experiences, concerns, and what you hope to achieve through therapy. We’ll take the time to understand your needs and expectations, creating a supportive space where you can share openly and move at your own pace.

    Shaped around you, never a fixed program

  3. Moving Forward Together

    Therapy is a collaborative journey. Together, we’ll identify meaningful goals, explore strategies that support your well-being, and build on your personal strengths. As we move forward, we’ll regularly reflect on your progress and adapt our approach to your evolving needs.

    Reviewed together as we go
Meaningful change

What Meaningful Progress Can Look Like.

  • Gain a deeper understanding of your emotions and recurring patterns.
  • Develop healthier ways to cope with stress and life’s challenges.
  • Build greater self-compassion, confidence, and emotional resilience.
  • Communicate your needs more clearly and establish healthier boundaries.
  • Navigate important decisions and life transitions with greater clarity.
  • Feel more connected to yourself, your values, and what truly matters to you.

Therapy doesn’t necessarily mean difficult emotions disappear completely. Often, progress means understanding them better and feeling more prepared to manage them when they come up.
