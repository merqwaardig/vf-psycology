Fees & reimbursement

Fair and transparent pricing for your specific case.

Because therapy is a personal and individualized process, the tariff is discussed during our free 10-minute call. This allows us to explore your situation, the type of support you need, and the best approach for your therapy.

Fees

The first call of 10 minutes is free and without obligation. Regular sessions typically last about 45 minutes; the duration can be adjusted based on individual needs and treatment plans. For detailed information about fees please contact me directly at vf.psychology@gmail.com or book a free 10-minute call where I can answer all your questions.

Insurance

I am a private psychologist and do not work with insurance companies in the Netherlands. The mental healthcare system is currently overwhelmed, with long waiting lists for insured care. By offering private therapy, I can guarantee immediate availability, so you can receive the support you need without unnecessary delays.
