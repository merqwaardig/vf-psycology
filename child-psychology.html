Child psychology

Is your child struggling with emotional regulation, anxiety, or behavioral challenges? Are you looking for the right guidance to support them? Specialized help is available to bring clarity and balance to your family.

Behavioral training for children. Behavioral training helps children develop essential skills to manage emotions, improve social interactions, and navigate daily challenges. This approach is especially beneficial for children experiencing impulsivity, emotional outbursts, or difficulties with routines and structure. Through structured techniques, I help children strengthen self-regulation and coping mechanisms, develop positive behaviors and emotional control, and improve focus, social skills, and daily habits.

Solution-Focused Therapy (SFT). This goal-oriented approach helps children and adolescents find practical solutions to their challenges rather than focusing solely on problems. By emphasizing strengths, resilience, and positive change, SFT empowers young individuals to take control of their emotional well-being: enhancing self-confidence and problem-solving skills, managing stress, anxiety, and peer relationships, and encouraging a positive mindset and personal growth.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT). CBT is one of the most effective approaches for treating anxiety, depression, and behavioral challenges in children and adolescents. This structured therapy helps young individuals understand how thoughts, feelings, and behaviors are connected and how they can make positive changes: managing anxiety, fears, and negative thinking patterns, improving emotional regulation and resilience, and developing healthy coping strategies for stress and social interactions.
