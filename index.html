Mission & approach

A safe, supportive, and understanding space.

My mission as a psychologist is to provide a safe, supportive, and understanding space where you can explore your emotions, overcome challenges, and grow into the best version of yourself. Whether you’re struggling with anxiety, self-esteem, depression, relationship difficulties, or personal development, I am here to guide you with compassion and professional expertise.

Client-centered & strength-based

Therapy is a collaborative process where your voice matters. I work alongside you to build on your strengths and create positive change.

Evidence-based techniques

I use scientifically supported methods such as CBT, SFT, and Behavioral Therapy, ensuring that you receive effective, results-driven support.

A safe & supportive space

My priority is to provide a judgment-free, compassionate environment where you feel comfortable expressing yourself.

Flexible & culturally sensitive

I understand the complex emotions and challenges that come with cultural transitions, expatriate life, and personal growth. My practice is open to people of all backgrounds, and sessions are available in Spanish and English.

At VF-Psychology, I believe that therapy should be adapted to each individual’s unique needs and circumstances. My approach is personalized, flexible, and goal-oriented, ensuring that every client receives the support that best fits their situation.

The therapeutic process

Three phases, reviewed together as we go.

  1. Initial evaluation

    During these sessions, we discuss the reasons for consultation and explore the challenges you are facing, identify possible underlying causes of emotional or behavioral difficulties, and use structured assessments, guided conversations, and small tasks to gain a deeper insight into your situation. Together we develop a preliminary plan for therapy based on your needs.

    Starts with a free 30-minute intake

  2. Treatment & counseling

    Once we have a clear understanding of your concerns, we begin the treatment phase. A personalized therapy plan is created, setting clear and achievable objectives. Regular progress evaluations ensure that therapy is effective and aligned with your goals, and adjustments are made when necessary to optimize the therapeutic process.

    Sessions of about 45 minutes

  3. Review & next steps

    Therapy is a journey, and progress is constantly reviewed. Once initial objectives have been met, we assess whether additional sessions are needed, provide tools and strategies for long-term emotional well-being, and ensure that you feel confident in applying what you’ve learned in your daily life.

    Number of sessions varies per person

I am a private psychologist and do not work with insurance companies in the Netherlands. The mental healthcare system is currently overwhelmed, with long waiting lists for insured care. By offering private therapy, I can guarantee immediate availability, so you can receive the support you need without unnecessary delays.

My fees are accessible, and some families may be able to finance their sessions through the Personal Budget (PGB) provided by the municipality.

The PGB (Persoonsgebonden Budget) is a subsidy that allows individuals with specific care needs to hire professionals of their choice, rather than relying solely on public healthcare services. If you meet the requirements, you can apply for this budget through your municipality and use it to cover the cost of therapy sessions.
