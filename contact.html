This practice isn’t an emergency service and I can’t respond quickly to urgent messages. If you’re in immediate danger, call 112. In the Netherlands you can reach 113 Suicide Prevention free, day or night, on 0800 0113 or at 113.nl. Outside the Netherlands, contact your local emergency number or crisis line.

What happens next

The therapeutic process.

  1. Initial evaluation

    We discuss the reasons for consultation, explore the challenges you are facing, and develop a preliminary plan for therapy based on your needs.

  2. Treatment & counseling

    A personalized therapy plan is created, setting clear and achievable objectives, with regular progress evaluations along the way.

  3. Review & next steps

    Once initial objectives have been met, we assess whether additional sessions are needed and make sure you feel confident applying what you’ve learned in daily life.
