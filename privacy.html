I have a professional duty of confidentiality. There are two situations where it can be broken: where there is an acute and serious risk to your safety or someone else’s, and where a court legally compels disclosure. Wherever it is possible, I will discuss it with you before anything is shared.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask me to:

  • show you what data I hold about you (access)
  • correct anything that is wrong (rectification)
  • delete data, where no legal retention duty applies (erasure)
  • limit what I do with it while a dispute is resolved (restriction)
  • send you a copy in a portable format (portability)
  • stop processing based on legitimate interest (objection)
  • withdraw consent you gave earlier, without it affecting what was lawful before

Write to privacy@vf-psychology.nl. I’ll respond within one month, and I may ask you to confirm your identity first so that I’m not handing your file to someone else.

Complaints

If you think I’ve handled your data badly, please tell me first — most of it is fixable in a conversation. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.
